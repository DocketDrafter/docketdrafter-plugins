# Corpus coverage and currency

The DocketDrafter statute and court-rules plugins contain primary legal text for research in
Claude. They generally omit annotations, editorial commentary, historical credits, and some
related materials. Always verify current law and time-sensitive procedural requirements against
an official source.

## U.S. Code

Derived from the Office of the Law Revision Counsel preliminary release checked August 6, 2026.
The latest bulk release available on that date was current through Public Law 119-102, July 12,
2026. It includes statutory section text but omits source credits, amendment notes,
effective-date notes, and other editorial material.

## New York Laws

Includes New York statutes, the New York State Constitution, the New York City Administrative
Code, and selected New York court rules. The statutory library was refreshed August 6, 2026. The
NYC Administrative Code is current through amendments effective May 17, 2026, and the court-rule
snapshots were generated June 27, 2026. Title 22 NYCRR coverage is limited to the court-rule
sources described by the plugin.

## Florida Laws

Includes the Florida Statutes, Florida Constitution, and statewide Florida court rules. The
statutes and Constitution were refreshed in August 2026 from official Florida Legislature and
Florida Senate sources. Statutory and constitutional annotations are omitted.

## Indiana Laws

Includes the Indiana Code and Indiana Constitution. The statutory library was retrieved August
10, 2026, from the Indiana General Assembly's 2026 Code publication and contains 83,148 sections
across Titles 1–37, including Title 7.1. The Constitution is the General Assembly's publication
as amended through 2024.

## Illinois Laws

Includes 72,163 sections across 2,813 Illinois Compiled Statutes Acts and 390 Illinois Supreme
Court Rules. The ILCS library is the newest official static ILGA snapshot available as of August
12, 2026: it was updated November 21, 2025, with Public Acts through 104-433. The Supreme Court
Rules are from the official compiled PDF current May 29, 2026. Recent enactments may not yet
appear in the static ILCS snapshot.

## Ohio Laws

Includes 33,220 sections of the Ohio Revised Code and 227 provisions of the Ohio Constitution.
The library was retrieved August 13, 2026, from the official Ohio Laws website maintained by the
Ohio Legislative Service Commission. It preserves effective dates, latest-legislation metadata,
official section links, and authenticated PDF links where available. The Revised Code is updated
continuously, so consult the live official source for time-sensitive questions.

## Federal Court Rules

Includes 2,570 rules across 28 selected rule sets: the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, Federal
Rules of Appellate Procedure, Federal Rules of Evidence, supplemental Admiralty and Social
Security rules, and selected district local and ECF rules from Arizona, California, Florida,
Illinois, New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, and Texas.

Coverage is selected rather than comprehensive, and each rule set has its own effective-through
date. It does not include the Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure, Federal Rules of Bankruptcy
Procedure, every federal district, standing orders, or judge-specific practices. Verify the live
court website before relying on a rule for a filing, deadline, or other time-sensitive matter.

## Provenance

The version-controlled legal data and the scripts used to build it are public in
[docketdrafter-plugin-content](https://github.com/DocketDrafter/docketdrafter-plugin-content).
Published releases identify the exact source commit and include a SHA-256 checksum.
